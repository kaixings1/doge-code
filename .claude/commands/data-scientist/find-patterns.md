# /find-patterns — Pattern Discovery

Discover natural segments, anomalies, and hidden patterns in your data.

## Invocation

```
/find-patterns Are there natural user segments in this engagement data?
/find-patterns [upload file] Find anomalies and unusual patterns
/find-patterns What patterns exist in our purchase behavior data?
```

## Workflow

### Step 1: Segment Discovery
Apply **segment-analysis** skill — find natural groupings.

### Step 2: Anomaly Detection
Apply **anomaly-detection** skill — flag unusual points and patterns.

### Step 3: Association Analysis
Look for co-occurrence patterns and conditional dependencies.

### Step 4: Report
Synthesize findings into actionable insights.

Offer follow-up:
- "Want to **profile specific segments** further?"
- "Should I **build a model** to predict segment membership?"
- "Want to **visualize** these patterns with /visualize?"
