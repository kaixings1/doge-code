# Revenue Analytics

## Purpose
Analyze revenue patterns, unit economics, and pricing for data-driven business decisions.

## Key Metrics

### SaaS Metrics
- **MRR/ARR**: Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: Expansion - Churn - Contraction
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: Target >3:1, payback <12 months
- **Gross Margin**: Revenue - COGS

### Revenue Decomposition
- New MRR (new customers)
- Expansion MRR (upgrades, cross-sell)
- Contraction MRR (downgrades)
- Churn MRR (cancellations)
- Net New MRR = New + Expansion - Contraction - Churn

### Unit Economics
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by channel
- LTV by segment
- Payback period
- Contribution margin per customer

## Usage Examples

```
"Build an MRR waterfall chart and calculate our unit economics"
```

## Output Format

- **Revenue Dashboard**: MRR waterfall, growth trends
- **Unit Economics**: LTV, CAC, payback by segment
- **Python Code**: Revenue calculations and visualizations
